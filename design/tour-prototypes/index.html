Tour page prototypes

Ten designs of the same tour page, one theme each. Same content in every file; see BRIEF.md.

  1. 01Antique AtlasOld landmarks: parchment, compass rose, wax-seal stamps, dotted route.
  2. 02Trail BlazesHiking: topo lines, painted trail blazes, wooden signposts, summit flag.
  3. 03Sunny PostcardSeaside holiday: postcards, stamps, postmarks, beach bunting.
  4. 04Metro LinesCity: a transit map with colored lines and stations.
  5. 05Hop-on Hop-offSightseeing bus: route stops, tickets, a bus at the current stop.
  6. 06Retro Travel PosterNational-park posters: flat landscapes, screen-print palette, badges.
  7. 07PassportTravel documents: visa stamps as progress, forms for decisions.
  8. 08Museum Audio GuideGallery walls in bold colors, wall labels, numbered stops.
  9. 09DeparturesAirport: split-flap board, boarding passes, wayfinding signs.
  10. 10Theme Park MapThe most playful: candy lands, ride tickets, "you are here" pin.
